          Danger zone

          Destroying the lab removes its running devices from the VM. Removing the lab from the manager does not touch the running devices or your saves.

          Apply to devices

          Choose which devices to review the generated plan against. A support host or a device the plan could not map is listed but cannot be applied to.

          Reviewing devices

          Each device is contacted and the change is tried inside its own transaction, then aborted. Nothing is applied.

          Closing this window does not stop the review. It keeps running; reopen Apply to devices to see it. A finished review stays available for 10 minutes; after that, review again.

          The device undoes the change by itself if the manager cannot confirm it within this time.
