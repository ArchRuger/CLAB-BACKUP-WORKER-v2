# Findings of the verification passes, and what was done about each

Written by the lead from the workers' reports (their own findings files could not be written by them).
Sources: the fixture pass at four widths on the tree `2578f22` (QA, one independent worker per width), the
"try to get blocked" pass in the fixture (B), the final parity check (P2), the guides check (D4), the tools
worker (T1) and the live browser pass on dev2 (L2, [../LIVE-ENV.md](../LIVE-ENV.md) section 10). The fixes
are the commits `F1:` of the task branch; the evidence under `1440/`, `1280/`, `760/` and `390/` was
regenerated by `tools/integration/evidence_pass.py` on the fixed tree. The exact steps of each finding are in
[../briefs/page-fix-list.md](../briefs/page-fix-list.md) and, for B, in the screenshots under `blocked/`.

**What held everywhere:** every state reached with zero console errors, page errors and failed requests
(118 states at 1440 and 1280; 117 of 118 and 112 of 114 at 390 and 760 before the fixes, because of Q390-01 and
Q760-04; 118 at all four widths after them); in every row of PROMPT 6.2 the person ends in a saved place or one
question with buttons; **New folder…** is enabled in every chooser state; no folder choice was refused in about
350 attacks; the friction budget is met at every width (first save 2 clicks and nothing typed, later save 2,
load a lab state 3, change folder 4, save as a lab state 3 plus the name or 4 with a one-click name).
